Public-domain US Census Bureau data, built by scripts/build-zcta.py.
Centroids: 2024 Gazetteer national ZCTA file (GEOID, INTPTLAT, INTPTLONG).
Place names: 2020 ZCTA-to-place relationship, largest incorporated place in each ZCTA (else the largest place). Census place suffixes such as "city" and "town" are removed.
Each NNN.json holds ZCTAs whose code starts with those three digits: [lat, lon] or [lat, lon, place, state].
