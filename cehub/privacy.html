CE Hub 2027 ("CE Hub", "we") is operated by [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME] at www.eyelovetravel.com/cehub. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What you can use without an account

You can browse every listing without signing in. Favorites, licenses and transcript entries you add while signed out are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us.

What we store when you sign in

  • Your email address, used only to send your sign-in links.
  • Data you choose to enter: favorited meetings, the state licenses you track and their renewal dates, a home location for distance estimates, and CE course records you add or upload (course titles, dates, hours, providers, formats).

Uploaded transcript PDFs are read in your browser and are not sent to us; only the course rows you import are saved. We do not collect license numbers, and you should not enter patient information.

How we use it

Only to show your data back to you in CE Hub. We do not sell your data, share it with sponsors or advertisers, or use it for marketing without your separate consent.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and protected by rules that let only your signed-in account read or change it. The site is hosted by IONOS.

Suggestions

If you send a meeting suggestion, we store what you type to review it. It may be used to add or correct a public listing.

Your choices

You can delete your saved data at any time from My licenses & CE → Your account → Delete my saved data, or email [CONTACT EMAIL] to delete your account entirely.

Sponsored listings
